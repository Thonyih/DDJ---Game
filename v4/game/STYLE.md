# Game Style

## In one sentence

A small, dark fantasy action RPG where a knight explores twelve lands inspired by Portuguese history
and folklore, seen from above in a chunky low-poly 3D style.

## Theme

- **Dark fantasy:** the world is grim and haunted: ghosts, skeletons, witches, graveyards and fog.
- **Portuguese folklore and history:** the places are real or legendary Portuguese locations
  (Tomar, Trancoso, Lisboa, Cabo das Tormentas) and the enemies come from local myths:
  the Lobisomem (werewolf), the Moura Encantada (enchanted Moorish maiden), Bruxas (witches),
  Diabretes (imps), and the giant **Adamastor** from Camões' *Os Lusíadas*.
- **The player is a knight** of a military order, taking work from the **Capitão da Ordem**.

## Look

- **Low-poly 3D:** simple, chunky shapes and flat colours rather than realistic detail. Characters
  are small and cartoon-proportioned (big heads, short bodies), which keeps the game readable from
  a distance and fits the free asset packs used (KayKit, Quaternius).
- **Isometric camera:** a fixed camera looks down at an angle and follows the knight, like classic
  action RPGs. The player always sees the area around them.
- **Getting darker toward the north:** the bottom row of maps is green, bright and calm. Each row
  north gets darker and colder: dimmer light, greyer ground, thicker fog and mist, more graves,
  bones and dead trees. The world visibly becomes more dangerous as the missions get harder.
- **Fog everywhere:** fog hides the edges of each map and drifts low over the ground, so the world
  feels larger and more mysterious than it is.

## Colours

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Interface frames and highlights | dark iron with **aged gold** |
| Danger, health, important buttons | **crimson** |
| Portuguese touch | **azulejo blue and white** tile strip on panels; the top sword and bow level glow azulejo blue |
| Southern lands | muted greens and browns |
| Northern lands | grey-greens, dusky blue-grey skies |

## Interface

- **Medieval book feel:** titles and buttons use **Cinzel** (a carved, Roman-style capital font);
  text uses **EB Garamond** (an old book font).
- **Panels** look like dark iron plates with gold borders, topped with an azulejo tile strip.
- **Kept out of the way:** only what is needed is on screen: resources top-left, map and compass
  top-right, health and weapons at the bottom. New players see even less, because the tutorial
  reveals each part of the interface when it becomes relevant.

## Sound

- **Ambience follows the map rows:** a calm forest in the south, then wind, then a dark drone, then
  a deep cavernous rumble in the far north.
- **Simple, physical effects:** steel swings, thuds, creaking chests, coins, page turns for menus
  and monster growls.

## Feel of play

- **Simple mouse controls:** left-click to walk, right-click to fight or interact.
- **Short, clear goals:** each mission is one task (defeat, find, or fetch).
- **Visible progress:** upgrades make the sword and bow bigger and change their colour, so getting
  stronger is something the player can see, not just a number.
- **Journey north:** the main motivation is pushing into darker, harder lands for better rewards,
  ending with the giant Adamastor.
