# Game Explainer — 2-page illustrated layout

This is a layout for a catchy 2-page explainer. Each page has short headline text plus numbered
**drawings** to create (and 3 real screenshots in `docs/images/`).

**Art direction for every drawing**
- **Style:** low-poly, chunky, cartoon proportions (big heads, short bodies), flat colours, soft
  shadows. Same feel as the game's KayKit characters.
- **Mood:** dark fantasy with a Portuguese twist: fog, graves and dead trees, gold accents, crimson
  highlights, azulejo blue-and-white tile details.
- **Palette:** dark iron greys, aged gold `#C9A45C`, crimson `#8E1B1B`, azulejo blue `#1F4E9C`,
  muted greens for the south, grey-blues for the north.
- **Text inside drawings:** Cinzel-style carved capitals. Keep text to a minimum.

## PAGE 1 — Enter the Realm

### Title banner

# **KNIGHTS OF TOMAR**
### *A dark fantasy adventure through the legends of Portugal*

> **DRAWING 1 — Hero banner (full width, top of page).**
> A small armoured knight with sword and shield stands on a misty hill, seen from behind, looking
> north over a landscape that turns from green fields (front) to dark graveyards and stormy cliffs
> (far back). At the horizon, the huge white silhouette of Adamastor rises from the fog.
> A thin azulejo tile border frames the banner.

### What is it?

You are a knight of a military order. Take missions from the **Capitão da Ordem**, fight creatures
from Portuguese folklore, find treasure, upgrade your gear and push north into darker lands,
all the way to the giant **Adamastor**.

**A browser game: just click and play.**

### A world of 12 lands

> **DRAWING 2 — The world map (left half of page).**
> A parchment-style map drawn as a 3 × 4 grid of 12 square lands, lettered A–L. The bottom row is
> bright green fields; each row up gets darker, foggier and grimmer (dead trees, graves, skulls),
> and the top row is near-black with storm clouds. A dotted path winds from **A** (bottom-left,
> "Start") up to the top. Small labels: *Tier 1 Easy … Tier 4 Deadly*. A compass rose in a corner
> with **N** pointing up.

| Row | Lands | Danger |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Campos de Tomar · Bosque dos Templários · Ruínas do Convento | Easy |
| 2 | Túneis de Tomar · Fronteira de Trancoso · Campos dos Cavaleiros Fantasma | Medium |
| 3 | Serra das Sombras · Caminho de Lisboa · Costa dos Navios Negros | Hard |
| 4 | Cabo das Tormentas · Ilhas dos Corsários · Costa do Além-Mar | Deadly |

**The further north, the darker the land, and the bigger the reward.**

### Simple controls

> **DRAWING 3 — Mouse controls (right half of page).**
> A big stylised mouse in the centre. The **left button** glows green with an arrow to a small
> knight walking toward a green ring on the ground: "WALK". The **right button** glows red with
> arrows to three scenes: the knight swinging at a skeleton ("FIGHT"), opening a glowing chest
> ("LOOT"), and talking to a crimson-armoured captain with a gold "!" over his head ("TALK").

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| **Left-click** | Walk |
| **Right-click** | Attack · open chests · talk · pick up items |
| **Slot 1 / Slot 2** | Sword or bow |

## PAGE 2 — The Adventure

### The game loop

> **DRAWING 4 — The loop (full width, circular diagram).**
> Five illustrated stops arranged in a circle with gold arrows between them:
> 1. **Talk** — the Capitão hands over a scroll.
> 2. **Fight or Explore** — the knight battles a werewolf / searches for glowing chests.
> 3. **Win** — the knight cheers, coins and spices burst out.
> 4. **Upgrade** — an inventory grid with a glowing, bigger sword.
> 5. **Head North** — the knight walks toward a darker, foggier land.
> In the centre: a small shield with the order's red cross.

**Talk → Fight → Win → Upgrade → Head North → repeat.**

### Monsters of Portuguese legend

> **DRAWING 5 — Enemy line-up (full width, like a "wanted" poster row).**
> Six creatures side by side, each with a small name scroll beneath, ordered from small to huge:
> **Diabrete** (tiny red winged imp) · **Lobisomem** (grey werewolf on all fours) ·
> **Bruxa** (skeleton witch with a pointed hat) · **Templário Perdido** (skeleton knight with a
> horned helm) · **Moura Encantada** (glowing blue ghostly hooded maiden) · **Mapinguari** (blocky
> stone-like beast). Behind them all, towering above the row and cut off by the page edge:
> **Adamastor**, a giant white horned demon with bat wings and a trident.

**Ghosts, witches, werewolves and a giant from *Os Lusíadas*.**

### 11 missions in 3 flavours

> **DRAWING 6 — Three mission cards (left half).**
> Three tilted parchment cards with wax seals:
> - **DEFEAT** — crossed swords over a skull. *"Defeat 3 Lobisomem."*
> - **SEARCH** — an open chest spilling gold. *"Find 2 lost chests."*
> - **TRAVEL** — a gold star on a mini map with a floating relic. *"Recover the Templar Relic."*

### Get stronger — and see it

> **DRAWING 7 — Sword evolution (right half).**
> Four swords side by side, growing in size: **Lv 1** plain steel · **Lv 2** bright steel ·
> **Lv 3** gilded gold · **Lv 4** large blade glowing azulejo blue. Below, a smaller matching row
> of four bows. Small coin and spice-pouch icons with rising prices: *1 → 5 → 25 gold*.

**Sword, bow and armour: 4 levels each. Every upgrade costs ×5 more, and you can see it in your hands.**
